TOS

        
Terms of Service

        
By placing a commission, you agree to the terms below. Please read everything carefully so we can have a smooth and magical collab!

      

      

        

          
JUMP TO A SECTION

          
          
          
          
          
          
          
          
        

        

          
1 Contact & Work Process

            
I only accept commissions through Twitter DMs or Gmail: crabbie.art@gmail.com

            
My working hours are 03:00–14:00 UTC/GMT, and I usually reply within 48 hours.

            
If you pay via PayPal, I'll send you a proper invoice to keep things secure and organized.

            
I reserve the right to decline a request if it doesn't suit my style, time, or comfort level.

            
When placing your order, please include:

            
  • Image refs
  • Pose/emotion
  • Canvas size
  • Deadline (if any)
  • Usage purpose

          

          
2 Payment & Refund Policy

            
100% upfront payment is required before I start. For large projects, we can split it into 2 payments upon agreement.

            
PayPal only. I don't accept other payment methods.

            
I will hold your slot for 4 days after confirmation. If no payment is made, the slot will be released.

            
Refund policy:

            
StageRefund

              
Not started yetFull refund

              
Sketch completed50% refund

              
Lineart or coloring doneNo refund

              
If I can't finish the artworkFull refund

            

            
If you send the wrong amount or there's a payment error, I'll do my best to help. However, I'm not responsible for incorrect PayPal emails.

            
Tax notice

              As a freelance artist based in Vietnam, I am required to pay a 5% personal income tax on all earnings.
              Therefore, a 5% tax fee will be added on top of the total invoice amount. This helps me stay
              compliant with local tax laws while continuing to provide quality work as an independent creator.
            

          

          
3 Edits & Feedback

            
Rough sketch
Unlimited changes.

            
Clean sketch
Up to 2 free revisions.

            
After coloring
Only small tweaks if possible. Major changes will come with an extra fee.

            
I don't accept vague feedback like "make it cuter" or "cooler" unless it's explained clearly. Please give specific instructions, e.g. "change the star to a heart" or "add a bow."

            
If I don't receive feedback within 5 days, I'll move forward with the current version and no further changes will be accepted.

          

          
4 Turnaround Time & Deadlines

            
Chibi commissions (emotes, badges, panels, alerts): 2–3 weeks

            
Normal/Anime style: around 1.5 months, depending on complexity → I'll give a more accurate ETA when we finalize your commission.

            
Rush orders are available for +20% extra.

            
If I'm over 10 days late without a valid reason:

            
  • I'll give a 10% refund and continue the work.
  • You can request a full refund, and I'll stop right away.
  • I might also include a little apology gift if we agree on it.

            
If you go silent for over 10 days:

            
  • I'll still save your slot in case you return, but no refund will be issued.

          

          
5 Files & Formats

            
You'll receive:

            
  • Transparent PNG
  • PNG with a colored background (if applicable)
  • Simple layered PSD (if your order includes one)

            
Files will be sent via Google Drive or email.

            
High-res files for printing are only provided for commercial use.

            
CMYK files are available upon request.

            
Watermarked previews may be posted on my socials, but your final file will be clean.

            
Default canvas sizes:

            
    
              
  • Emotes/Badges: 1167px, then resized for Twitch/Discord
    • 
              
  • Chibi Alerts: 1680px
    • 
              
  • Chibi Panels: ~920px
    • 
              
  • Chibi/Anime Illust: based on layout, usually around A4 size or larger
    • 
            

            
Custom size requests are welcome!

            
PSDs with full layer separation are only provided for animation use and may include an extra fee.

            
I'll store your files for 3–6 months; after that, I may no longer have them.

          

          
6 What I Don't Draw

            
As for specific content (characters, styles, themes), I'll decide case by case. I always try my best to adapt as long as it's within my comfort zone.

          

          
7 Copyright & Usage Rights

            
I retain full copyright of the artwork unless a transfer is agreed upon (additional fee required).

            
You may use the commissioned art for personal use only: avatars, overlays, banners, etc.

            
You may NOT:

            
  • Print or sell the artwork, or use it commercially without proper licensing.
  • Repost without credit.
  • Use for AI/NFT/filter modifications.

            
I do not allow my work to be used for AI training, AI blending, or filtered edits that distort the original intent.

            
Commercial use
Requires an additional x2 of the base price.

            
Required credit
"Art by @crbbie"

            
If you violate copyright, I reserve the right to file DMCA claims and publicly address the issue.

          

          
8 Artist Rights

            
I may post finished artwork on my social media or use it in my portfolio unless marked as a private commission.

            
Private commissions have a +20% fee since I won't be using them for promo purposes.

            
I reserve the right to cancel the commission and issue a refund if the client is disrespectful or places unfair demands.

            
I may slightly adjust the layout or style during the process for better quality, as long as it aligns with the original brief.

          

          

            
Thanks for reading all the way through!

            
Feel free to message me if you have any questions or just wanna say hi~

            
Let's make cute magic together!

            — Crabbie
